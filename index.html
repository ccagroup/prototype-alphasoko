Frequently asked.

Is AlphaSoko free to use?
Yes. The core platform — budgeting, M-Pesa analysis, calculators and AI coach — is free forever. Premium investment features are available on a transparent monthly plan.
Is my M-Pesa data safe?
Absolutely. We use 256-bit AES encryption, never store your Safaricom credentials, and all statement processing happens in isolated, encrypted environments. You can delete your data any time.
Do you give financial advice?
No. AlphaSoko is an educational and analytical tool. All insights are for informational purposes. Always consult a CMA-licensed advisor before making investment decisions.
Can I connect my bank account?
Direct bank connections are coming soon. For now, you can import CSV statements from any Kenyan bank and all M-Pesa statements.
Will AlphaSoko work outside Kenya?
AlphaSoko is built Kenya-first. We're expanding to Uganda, Tanzania and Rwanda in 2027, with pan-African coverage by 2028.

How it works

Treasury Bills are discount instruments issued by the Central Bank of Kenya. You buy below face value and get the full face value at maturity.

Discount = Face × (Yield × Days / 365)
Price = Face − Discount
Withholding tax = Interest × 15%

Financial glossary

Quick definitions
Treasury Bill (T-Bill)

Short-term government debt (91, 182 or 364 days) sold at a discount to face value.

Treasury Bond

Long-term government debt (2–25 years) that pays fixed coupons every 6 months.

MMF (Money Market Fund)

A low-risk fund that pools money to invest in short-term instruments like T-Bills.

DhowCSD

The Central Bank of Kenya's digital platform for buying and holding government securities.

NSE (Nairobi Securities Exchange)

Kenya's stock exchange where shares of public companies are traded.

CMA (Capital Markets Authority)

The regulator of Kenya's capital markets, licensing brokers and protecting investors.

AlphaSoko Ltd ("we", "our", "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use and safeguard your personal data when you use our platform.

2. Information we collect

  • Account information: name, email, phone number.

3. How we use your information

We use your data to provide the AlphaSoko service, generate insights, improve the product and comply with legal obligations. We never sell your data to third parties.

Under the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019, you have the right to access, correct, delete or export your data. Contact us at privacy@alphasoko.com.
